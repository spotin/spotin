# Spot in®

The platform for public institutions (municipalities, regions, museums,
libraries, tourist offices) to create and manage QR codes that never expire.

- **Printed once, valid forever.** QR codes lead to Spot in®, and their
  destination can change at any time without reprinting them.
- **Content you control.** Each QR code represents a place or an entity, with
  its own page, or redirects to the website of your choice.
- **A clear framework.** Each organization decides who creates and manages its
  QR codes, with the roles of responsible and member.
